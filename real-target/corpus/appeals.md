# Appeals and Fair Hearings

This page explains how to challenge a decision about your benefits. All details on this
page are illustrative and synthetic.

You can ask for a fair hearing to challenge a decision about your benefits. A fair
hearing is a meeting where you can explain why you disagree with the decision. You can
request a fair hearing if your application is denied or if your benefits are reduced. You
must request a fair hearing within 90 days of the date on your notice.

You can request a fair hearing online, by mail, or by calling the benefits office. You
may bring a representative, such as a friend, a relative, or a legal aid worker, to help
you. If you request a hearing before a reduction takes effect, your benefits may
continue at the current amount until the hearing decision is made.
