# Applying for Benefits

You can apply for benefits online, by mail, or in person at a local benefits office.
You will need to provide the following documents: proof of identity, proof of income, and proof of residence.
A caseworker reviews every application.

Applications are processed within 30 days of submission. If your application is
approved, your benefits begin on the first day of the following month. If your
application is denied, the notice will explain how to appeal within 90 days.

Benefits must be renewed every 12 months. You will receive a renewal notice by mail
before your benefits expire.
