# Benefit Amounts and How You Receive Them

This page explains how much you may receive from the Community Benefits Program and
how benefits are issued. All figures on this page are illustrative and synthetic.

Benefits are loaded onto an Electronic Benefits Transfer (EBT) card each month. A
household of one may receive up to $300 per month. Households with more members may
receive a higher monthly amount. Your exact amount depends on your household size and
income.

Your benefits are added to your EBT card on the same day each month, based on the last
digit of your case number. You can check your balance online or by calling the number
on the back of your card. Unused benefits roll over to the next month, but benefits
expire if the card is not used for 12 months.
