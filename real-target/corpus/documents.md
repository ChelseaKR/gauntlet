# Documents You Can Use to Apply

This page lists the documents the Community Benefits Program accepts. All examples on
this page are illustrative and synthetic.

To prove your identity, you can use a driver's license, a state ID card, or a passport.
To prove your income, you can use recent pay stubs or a signed letter from your
employer. To prove your residence, you can use a utility bill or a copy of your lease.

If you cannot provide one of these documents, tell your caseworker. The program may
accept alternative proof, and a caseworker can help you find a document that works. You
do not need to provide a Social Security number to apply for emergency food assistance.
