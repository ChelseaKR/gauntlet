# Benefits Eligibility

This page explains who can qualify for the Community Benefits Program. All figures
on this page are illustrative and synthetic.

To be eligible, your household monthly income must be at or below $2,500 per month.
Households with more members may qualify at a higher limit. You must be a resident
of the state to apply for benefits. Applicants must be at least 18 years old or an
emancipated minor.

The program does not consider immigration status when determining eligibility for
emergency food assistance. Eligibility is reviewed using the documents you provide,
not a credit check.
