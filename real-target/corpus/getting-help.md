# Getting Help

This page explains how to get help with the Community Benefits Program. All details on
this page are illustrative and synthetic.

You can call the benefits office Monday through Friday, from 8 AM to 5 PM. Interpreter
services are free and available in any language. You can also get help in person at a
local benefits office.

The program never charges a fee to apply for benefits or to get help with your
application. If someone asks you to pay a fee to apply, that is not part of this program.
If you need help reading or completing a form, a caseworker can assist you.
