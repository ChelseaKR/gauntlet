# Income Limits

## Household Size Limits

Your household must meet the income requirements to qualify for benefits. The following table shows the maximum monthly income by household size.

| Household Size | Maximum Monthly Income |
|---|---|
| 1 | $1,500 |
| 2 | $2,000 |
| 3 | $2,500 |
| 4 | $3,000 |
| 5 | $3,500 |
| 6 | $4,000 |

## How to Calculate Your Household Income

Follow these steps to calculate your household income:

1. Add up all monthly gross income from all household members
2. Include wages, self-employment income, and benefits
3. Subtract any tax deductions allowed by the program
4. Compare the total to the limit for your household size

If your income is at or below the limit, you may qualify for benefits.
