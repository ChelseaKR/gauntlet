# Overpayments

This page explains what happens if you are paid more benefits than you should have
received. All details on this page are illustrative and synthetic.

An overpayment happens when you receive more benefits than your household qualified for,
for example because a change was not reported on time. If you are overpaid, you will
receive a notice that explains the amount and how to repay it.

You can repay an overpayment in monthly installments, and a caseworker can help you set
up a payment plan you can afford. If you disagree that you were overpaid, you can request
a fair hearing within 90 days of the date on the overpayment notice.
