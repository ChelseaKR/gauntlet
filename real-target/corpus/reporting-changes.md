# Reporting Changes

After you are approved, you must report certain changes to keep your benefits
accurate. You must report changes within 10 days of the change.

Changes you must report include a change in household income, a change of address,
and a change in the number of people living in your household. You can report a
change online or by calling the benefits office. Failing to report a change on time
may result in an overpayment that you have to repay.
