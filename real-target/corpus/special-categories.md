# Special Categories of Applicants

This page explains rules for specific groups of applicants to the Community Benefits
Program. All rules on this page are illustrative and synthetic.

Applicants who are elderly, meaning age 60 or older, or who have a disability may
qualify with a higher income limit and may use simplified reporting. Full-time students
may qualify if they work at least 20 hours per week or care for a young child.

A household can include both eligible and ineligible members. The eligible members can
still receive benefits. The benefit is calculated using only the eligible members, and a
caseworker can explain how this applies to your household.
