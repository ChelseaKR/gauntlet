# Work Requirements

This page explains the work requirements for the Community Benefits Program. All rules
on this page are illustrative and synthetic.

Some adults must meet a work requirement to keep their benefits. The work requirement is
at least 20 hours per week of work, job training, or approved volunteer activity. It
applies to able-bodied adults who have no dependents.

Exemptions from the work requirement include being under 18 or over 60, being pregnant,
caring for a child under age 6, or having a disability. If you think an exemption applies
to you, tell your caseworker and provide any document that supports it.
